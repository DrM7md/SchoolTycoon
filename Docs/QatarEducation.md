# Qatar private-school reference (researched 2026-11-07)

> **ملخص عربي:** مرجع قواعد المدارس الخاصة في قطر (التقويم، الرسوم، القسائم، رخص المعلمين). البنود المعلّمة **[UNVERIFIED]** تُتحقق من موقع الوزارة قبل أن تصبح قواعد.

Real-world grounding for the long-term simulation. Mostly from news reports of MOEHE announcements (QNA, The Peninsula, Gulf Times). **[UNVERIFIED]** items must be checked on edu.gov.qa before they become rules.

## Structure and calendar
- Stages: KG1–KG2 (ages ~3–5), Primary G1–6, Preparatory G7–9, Secondary G10–12. International schools use their own names (UK Years, US grades, IB PYP/MYP/DP).
- MOEHE unified calendar 2025-26 → 2027-28; public schools reopened 31 Aug 2025; first-semester finals before National Day (18 Dec); extra Ramadan days off. [qna](https://qna.org.qa/en/News-Area/News/2025-7/8/moehe-approves-new-academic-calendar-for-20252028)
- Typical year [UNVERIFIED]: late Aug → June; 2 semesters (or 3 terms for British); winter break, Eid, spring break; Fri–Sat weekend; ~6–7 h day; Ramadan ~4–5 h; holidays National Day and Sports Day.

## Curricula and mandatory subjects
- Offered: Qatari national, British (IGCSE/A-Level), American (AP), IB, Indian (CBSE), Pakistani, Filipino, French, Arab-country curricula, community schools.
- All private schools (KG included since 2022-23) must teach **Arabic, Islamic Education, Qatar History**; reported hours **4 / 2 / 1 per week** (check currency). [Peninsula](https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/19/05/2021/Ministry-makes-Arabic-and-Islamic-education-compulsory-subjects-in-Qatar-private-schools) · [Gulf Times](https://gulf-times.com/story/643112/New-academic-guidelines-for-private-schools)

## Regulation
- Law No. 23 of 2015 (a new draft law was in consultation). Licences valid **3 years** (2025 system); bus/traffic approval **yearly**; Civil Defence approval required; yearly licensing window for new schools. [qna](https://qna.org.qa/en/News-Area/News/2025-10/12/moehe-launches-enhanced-licensing-system-for-private-schools-kindergartens)
- **Fees need MOEHE approval.** School Fees Policy 2026 (full effect 2027-28): increases linked to performance, **18 months' notice** to parents, service/optional fees also regulated. 2026-27 pilot: 99 applied, 54 approved, 22 rejected. [qna](https://qna.org.qa/en/News-Area/News/2026-6/12/moehe-launches-new-policy-regulating-private-school-fees-starting-from-2027-2028-academic-year)
- Inspection published on MOEHE portal; overall bands **Very Effective / Effective / Somewhat Effective / Ineffective**, domain scores out of 4 [secondary source]. Separate QNSA accreditation; national-identity value awards (Gold, Diamond).
- Class size ~30 max, KG teacher + assistant [UNVERIFIED]. Gender separation common in Arabic/national schools; international schools mostly co-ed [UNVERIFIED].

## Teachers
- MOEHE professional licence; tiers by experience: **Novice 3, Competent 6, Experienced 12, Expert 18 years**. Only ~40% licensed in 2022. [Peninsula](https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/15/07/2025/ministry-of-education-grants-1404-professional-licenses-to-academics-school-leaders)
- Bachelor + teaching qualification (PGCE/B.Ed), attested degrees, 2+ years' experience for international schools.
- Pay (job ads): Western-curriculum expats **QAR 11–16k/month + housing 5.5–11k**, flights, medical, end-of-service gratuity; Indian/community schools ~QAR 3–7k [UNVERIFIED].
- Expat sponsorship, 2-year contracts, gratuity ≥3 weeks' basic per year; hiring peaks Jan–Apr for August; age ceiling ~60 [UNVERIFIED]; turnover 15–25%/year [UNVERIFIED].

## Students and parents
- Annual tuition (2025-26 guides): Indian/community QAR 6–18k; mid British/American/Arabic ~20–45k; premium 45–90k; top tier up to ~100–125k. Plus registration deposits (1–5k), books, uniform, transport (~4–8k), exam fees.
- **Education voucher** for Qatari children: **QAR 28,000/year**; 2026: 3,793 fully covered places at 17 schools (family income ≤ QAR 35k/month). [Gulf Times](https://www.gulf-times.com/article/731109/community/education/moehe-offers-3793-fully-covered-places-for-qatari-students-at-17-private-schools-under-education-voucher-initiative)
- Admission guide 2026; waitlists and entrance tests at premium schools; sibling priority. A 2012 rule reserved 25% of seats in new schools for Qataris (current status unverified).
- Exams by curriculum: national finals, IGCSE, A-Level, IB DP, AP/SAT, CBSE boards; equivalency needed for local universities.

## Market
- ~352 private schools, ~228k students (Dec 2025); +48k students expected by 2029. Mostly expatriate families; large Indian sector; premium brands expanding; ministry incentives for investors.

## Candidate game systems
| Real rule | Game system |
| --- | --- |
| Fee approval tied to rating, 18-month notice | Yearly fee-request window; approval chance by inspection band; increase applies the year after next |
| Fee tiers | Fee bracket sets demand pool, nationality mix, parent expectations |
| 4-band inspection | Inspection every 1–2 years with domain scores; affects reputation, fee approval, voucher eligibility |
| Mandatory Arabic/Islamic/Qatar History | Timetable constraint; must hire scarce Arabic/Islamic teachers |
| Licence 3 years, bus yearly | Renewal events and yearly fleet inspection cost |
| Class cap ~30, KG assistants | Capacity = classrooms × cap; overcrowding hurts rating |
| Licence tiers 3/6/12/18 | Teacher experience → level and quality; licensing cost; unlicensed = inspection risk |
| Expat packages, gratuity | Salary = base + housing; gratuity on exit grows with tenure; veterans cost more but stay; young hires cheap but leave |
| Age ceiling ~60 | Teachers retire |
| Turnover | End-of-year resignations driven by pay vs market, workload, leadership |
| Voucher QAR 28k | Qatari applicants appear when school is approved and rated well; optional free seats for goodwill |
| Curriculum choice | Chosen at founding; sets fee ceiling, teacher source/cost, exam events, demographics |
| Calendar | Yearly cycle: August start, semesters/terms, Ramadan short days, Eid, exams, summer results, spring enrolment |
| Market growth | Rival schools open; demand grows by segment |
| Deposits and waitlists | Spring deposits; waitlist above capacity boosts reputation and fee approval |

**Verify on edu.gov.qa before implementing:** class-size/ratio caps, inspection framework, full 2026 fee policy and percentage caps, current subject hours, gender rules, teacher age/visa limits.
